## Purpose

To brief Executive Management on the **AI Digital Campus** initiative, present the **working prototype**
developed to de-risk it, and recommend the **way forward** for the Task Force Team's (TFT) feasibility
study.

## Background

The AI Digital Campus Initiative Task Force Team was established to review the initiative, conduct a
feasibility study, assess its alignment with organizational goals, and propose an action plan. To ground
that study in evidence rather than concept, a **functional prototype** of the proposed platform has
already been built and quality-assured in-house.

## What the AI Digital Campus is

An **internal AI/ML learning and innovation platform** — effectively "a private Kaggle for KOC" — where
employees **learn** data science through guided tracks, **build** models in a no-code/low-code visual
studio, and **compete** on challenges drawn from **real KOC problems** (electric submersible pump
failure, production analytics, reservoir, HSE). Submissions are scored automatically on withheld data,
ranked on live leaderboards, and recognized through a points-and-badges system that sustains engagement.
All data and models remain inside KOC.

## Why it matters

- **Capability at scale, not consultants** — upskill hundreds of employees on our own data, building a
  durable internal asset instead of renting expertise.
- **Real outcomes** — the competitions that train people also surface candidate models for genuine
  operational problems (e.g., predictive maintenance to reduce unplanned downtime).
- **Engagement and retention** — a modern, gamified, community-driven development experience.
- **Low cost, low risk, fully governed** — standard Microsoft technology, self-hosted, data-resident in
  Kuwait, with enterprise security and a complete audit trail. No per-seat SaaS fees; no data leaving KOC.

## Prototype status

Built and quality-gated in-house: full feature set, automated test suite passing, complete
user / administrator / developer documentation, intranet single sign-on, role-based access control,
org-scoped data visibility, passwordless database access, and a fail-fast production safety guard.
It is **ready to demonstrate and to pilot**.

## Recommended way forward

1. **Endorse a 90-day pilot** with two directorates (~[100–150] participants, 3–4 seeded competitions on
   business-nominated problems) as the empirical core of the TFT feasibility study.
2. **Nominate an executive sponsor** and pilot directorate leads.
3. **Confirm the target problem set** with the business so challenges map to real priorities.

The full white paper (`AI_DIGITAL_CAMPUS_WHITE_PAPER`) accompanies this memo with the business case,
feasibility assessment, architecture, security, and roadmap. I am available to brief the committee and
demonstrate the prototype at your convenience.
