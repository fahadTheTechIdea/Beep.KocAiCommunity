## 1. Purpose

Validate — with real users and real KOC problems — whether the AI Digital Campus drives **adoption**,
**capability growth**, and **business value**, and produce the evidence the Task Force Team needs to
recommend (or not) a company-wide rollout. The pilot is the empirical core of the feasibility study.

## 2. Objectives

1. Demonstrate **adoption and engagement** among a representative employee group.
2. Show measurable **capability growth** (from learner to competent model-builder).
3. Produce **≥1–2 candidate models** on business-nominated problems worth advancing.
4. Confirm the **operating model** (support, content, competition curation) is sustainable.
5. Surface risks, costs, and effort for an informed **go / no-go** decision.

## 3. Scope

- **Participants:** ~[100–150] employees across **[2] directorates** ([Directorate A], [Directorate B]),
  spanning grades and roles; no prior AI experience required.
- **Competitions:** **[3–4]** challenges on **business-nominated problems** (e.g., ESP failure
  prediction, production analytics), each with a labelled training set, an evaluation set, and a withheld
  answer key.
- **Learning:** a curated **["Getting Started" + 2–3 core tracks]** aligned to the competition tasks.
- **Hosting:** [intranet / Azure Kuwait Central]; Windows single sign-on; org-scoped data visibility.
- **Out of scope:** company-wide rollout, production deployment of models (winning models are
  *candidates* entering existing model governance), and directory-wide integration beyond the pilot.

| Dimension | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Adoption | Registered participants / invited | ≥ [70]% |
| Engagement | Active participants (≥1 submission or track lesson) | ≥ [50]% of registered |

| Capability | Participants making ≥1 scored submission | ≥ [Y] employees |
| Relevance | Competitions run on nominated problems | [3–4] |
| Value | Candidate models worth advancing to governance | ≥ [1–2] |

## 7. Participant onboarding

- **Launch webinar / clinics** introducing the platform and the first challenge.
- **Self-serve start:** sign in with the KOC account (no install), complete the Getting Started track,
  enter the featured competition.
- **Weekly office hours** and a **community channel** for questions and peer help.
- **Desktop Studio** available for hands-on classroom sessions and low-connectivity users.

## 8. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Endorsement, removing blockers, visibility. |
| TFT / Chair | Governance, decisions, evaluation, reporting. |
| Product owner (T&CD) | Day-to-day delivery, competition calendar, participant experience. |
| Platform administrator (IT) | Hosting, access, monitoring, support. |
| Subject-matter experts | Nominate problems; prepare/validate datasets and answer keys; judge relevance. |
| Directorate leads | Nominate participants; encourage engagement; review outcomes. |
| Secretary | Documentation, minutes, and the actions log. |

## 9. Resourcing (indicative)

- Part-time product owner + platform administrator for the pilot window.
- SME time for problem/dataset preparation (front-loaded in Weeks 1–2).
- Hosting on existing [intranet / Azure] capacity; no new licences (standard Microsoft stack).
- Recognition/prizes budget: [to confirm].

## 10. Communications plan

- **Pre-launch:** sponsor + directorate-lead briefings; establishment/announcement notices.
- **Launch:** employee announcement email; kickoff webinar.
- **During:** weekly leaderboard/standings updates; clinic reminders; community engagement.
- **Close:** results recap, recognition of top performers, and thank-you.

## 11. Data & security handling

- Only **approved, appropriately-scoped datasets** are used; answer keys remain withheld by the platform.
- **Org-scoped visibility** limits who can see each dataset/competition.
- All data and models **remain within KOC** (intranet / Azure Kuwait Central); full audit trail.
- Competition hosting is permission-gated and curated by SMEs.

## 12. Risks & mitigations (pilot-specific)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low enrolment / drop-off | Sponsor endorsement, directorate-lead nudges, clinics, recognition, easy self-serve start. |
| Dataset preparation delays | Front-load SME work in Weeks 1–2; keep a backup problem ready. |
| Uneven skill levels | Getting Started track + office hours + AutoML fast path for beginners. |
| Support load | Community channel, FAQ/help, scheduled clinics rather than ad-hoc. |
| Inconclusive results | Pre-agree the success measures and the decision criteria before launch. |

## 13. Decision gate (go / no-go)

At the end of Week 13, the TFT recommends one of: **Scale** (proceed to company-wide rollout),
**Iterate** (extend/adjust and re-measure), or **Stop**. The recommendation is evidence-based against the
Section 4 success measures and the risk findings.

## 14. Deliverables

1. Pilot report (results vs. success measures; qualitative feedback).
2. Shortlist of candidate models for model governance.
3. Go/no-go recommendation and, if scaling, an outline company-wide rollout plan.
