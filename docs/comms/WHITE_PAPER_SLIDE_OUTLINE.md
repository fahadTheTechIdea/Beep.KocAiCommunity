# AI Digital Campus — Management Presentation Outline

A slide-by-slide outline for presenting the white paper to executive management. Target length:
**~15–18 minutes** plus discussion. Each slide lists the on-screen points and a short speaker note.
Aim for one clear idea per slide.

---

### Slide 1 — Title
**AI Digital Campus** — An internal AI/ML learning & innovation platform for KOC.
*Prepared by the AI Digital Campus Task Force Team · KOC Internal · [Date].*
> **Note:** Set the frame — "how KOC builds AI capability in-house, on our own data."

### Slide 2 — The bottom line (lead with the ask)
- Build broad AI capability **in-house**, on KOC data — not through consultants.
- A **working prototype already exists**; we're asking to **endorse a 90-day pilot**.
> **Note:** Executives appreciate the recommendation up front; the rest is support.

### Slide 3 — The challenge
- AI capability is concentrated in a few specialists and external vendors.
- Insight — and our data — too often leaves the company.
- Digital transformation needs **broad** AI literacy, not a handful of experts.
> **Note:** Establish the gap before the solution.

### Slide 4 — The idea: Learn → Build → Compete
- A single journey: learn AI, build models (no coding), compete on real problems.
- "A private, secure **Kaggle for KOC**" — engaging, measurable, purposeful.
> **Note:** The competition model is why skills actually stick and transfer to the job.

### Slide 5 — What we built (demo moment)
- A functional platform: Home, Learn, the Arena (competitions), the Studio, Community, Admin.
- **Live demo:** featured competition → leaderboard → build a model in the Studio.
> **Note:** 3–4 minutes of live demo here is the most persuasive part. Keep it crisp.

### Slide 6 — Capabilities at a glance
- **Learn** guided tracks · **Compete** on real KOC problems · **Build** in a visual/AutoML Studio.
- Community, gamification (points/badges/leaderboards), and a full admin console.
> **Note:** Breadth without drowning them in detail.

### Slide 7 — Real KOC problems
- Competitions on ESP failure prediction, production analytics, reservoir, HSE.
- Scored automatically on withheld data — only modelling skill moves a rank.
> **Note:** Tie learning directly to operational value.

### Slide 8 — Secure & sovereign
- Windows single sign-on; role-based access; org-scoped data visibility.
- **Data and models never leave KOC** — intranet or Azure Kuwait Central.
- Passwordless database access; full audit trail; production safety guard.
> **Note:** Pre-empt the security/data-residency question — it's already handled.

### Slide 9 — Standard, low-cost technology
- Built on standard Microsoft **.NET** — self-hosted, maintainable, no per-seat SaaS fees.
- Containerized for IIS intranet or Azure; CI quality gate.
> **Note:** Low cost and low risk; reuses skills IT already has.

### Slide 10 — Alignment with KOC goals
- Capability & nationalization · digital transformation · knowledge retention · safety & efficiency.
> **Note:** Map to the corporate objectives your audience owns.

### Slide 11 — Feasibility & risks
- Technically proven by the prototype; organizationally coherent.
- Key risks (adoption, data sensitivity, sustainment) with clear mitigations.
> **Note:** Show it's been thought through; honesty builds credibility.

### Slide 12 — Value & target outcomes
- Reach [300–500] employees in year one; [6–8] competitions/year on nominated problems.
- [2–3] competition-derived models into the model-governance pipeline.
- One predictive-maintenance win can pay for the initiative many times over.
> **Note:** Label these as **targets to validate in the pilot**, not promises.

### Slide 13 — Roadmap
- Phase 0 Prototype (done) → **Phase 1 Pilot (90 days)** → Hardening → Company-wide → Sustain.
> **Note:** Emphasize the low-commitment, high-learning pilot step.

### Slide 14 — Governance & operating model
- Governed by the TFT; operated by T&CD with IT support; winning models enter existing model governance.
> **Note:** Clarify who runs it after the study.

### Slide 15 — The ask
- **Endorse the 90-day pilot** (2 directorates, ~[100–150] participants, 3–4 competitions).
- **Nominate an executive sponsor** and pilot directorate leads.
- **Confirm the target problem set** with the business.
> **Note:** Close on the three specific, easy decisions.

### Backup slides (for Q&A)
- B1 — Architecture diagram / component overview.
- B2 — Security & data-residency detail.
- B3 — Detailed pilot plan and success measures.
- B4 — Cost model and resourcing.
