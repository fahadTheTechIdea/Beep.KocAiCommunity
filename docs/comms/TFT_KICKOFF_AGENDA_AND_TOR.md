## Terms of Reference (ToR)

### 1. Purpose & mandate
The Task Force Team is established to review the **AI Digital Campus** initiative, conduct a **feasibility
study** to determine its applicability, analyze its **coherence and alignment with organizational goals**,
and **propose the way forward and an action plan**.

### 2. Scope
- **In scope:** assessment of the initiative concept and working prototype; alignment with KOC/T&CD
  objectives; feasibility (technical, organizational, security, cost); a recommended pilot; and an action
  plan for the way forward.
- **Out of scope:** full production build-out and company-wide deployment decisions, which follow
  endorsement of the TFT's recommendations.

### 3. Deliverables
1. Feasibility study report (applicability, alignment, options, risks).
2. Recommended way forward and action plan (including a proposed pilot).
3. Supporting materials (business case / white paper, prototype demonstration).

### 4. Proposed workstreams
- **A — Business & alignment:** objectives fit, target problems, expected outcomes/benefits.
- **B — Learning & content:** tracks, competition themes, subject-matter-expert engagement.
- **C — Technology & security:** architecture, hosting, identity, data residency, governance.
- **D — Operating model & rollout:** roles, support, pilot design, timeline, and measures of success.

### 5. Timeline
The TFT remains active for the duration of the study, targeted for completion **within three months**.
Indicative milestones: kickoff (Week 1) → workstream findings (Weeks 3–6) → draft report (Weeks 8–10) →
final report & recommendation (Weeks 11–12).

### 6. Roles & responsibilities
- **Chair (Head):** direction, decisions, and executive liaison.
- **Secretary:** agendas, minutes, action log, document control.
- **Members / workstream owners:** conduct assigned analysis and contribute findings.

### 7. Ways of working
- **Cadence:** [weekly / bi-weekly] progress meetings; ad-hoc workstream sessions as needed.
- **Decisions:** by consensus where possible; the Chair decides where consensus is not reached.
- **Documentation:** all outputs recorded and version-controlled by the Secretary.
