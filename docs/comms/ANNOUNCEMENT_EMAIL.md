Dear Colleagues,

We are excited to introduce the **AI Digital Campus** — a new internal platform where you can **learn
artificial intelligence and machine learning, build models with no coding required, and compete on real
KOC challenges** — all inside KOC, on your work account.

Think of it as **a private, secure "Kaggle" for KOC**: you pick up practical AI skills through guided
learning, apply them in a visual model-building studio, and put them to the test on competitions drawn
from genuine KOC problems — with a live leaderboard, recognition, and rewards.

## What you can do

- **Learn** — work through guided tracks that take you from the basics to building real models.
- **Build** — install **KOC Studio** on your PC and prepare data and train models with clicks, not code
  (AutoML does the heavy lifting when you want a fast start). It runs on your own machine, so your data
  stays there and a long training run does not depend on the network.
- **Compete** — enter challenges on real KOC problems (e.g., equipment failure prediction, production
  analytics), climb the live leaderboard, and earn points, badges, and recognition.
- **Connect** — discuss, ask questions, and share solutions with colleagues across the company.

## Why take part

- **Grow a future-ready skill** that's increasingly valuable across every part of KOC.
- **Get recognized** — your progress, badges, and standings are visible to you and your leaders.
- **Make a real impact** — the best models can help solve genuine operational challenges.
